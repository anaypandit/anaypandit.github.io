PROJECT STATUS

Pinnacle View → ASMSA

01

How it started

I helped launch and captain Pinnacle View Middle School’s robotics program. The work combined building robots with helping a team develop the habits to test, document, and improve together.

02

What I worked on

My work spans building, driving, strategy, and engineering documentation. At ASMSA, a concrete example was testing drivetrain acceleration, pushing strength, and stability—questions where a change in one part of the design affects the whole robot.

03

A season to build on

Pinnacle View’s three teams earned 21 team-awards during 2024–25: 10 for 49959Z, 10 for 49959P, and one for 49959C. These are program results across three teams, rather than 21 individual awards.

04

Arkansas State → Worlds

At the March 2025 Arkansas middle-school championship, 49959Z won Excellence, Tournament Champions, and Robot Skills Champion, qualifying for Worlds. 49959P earned Judges, and 49959C earned Sportsmanship.

05

The next team

At ASMSA, I have taken on rebuilding an existing program as a builder, driver, notebooker, and strategist. Its older 2021–23 awards predate my involvement. The current challenge is building a stronger process and team—not claiming the program’s earlier results as my own.

06

What this has taught me

A robot is only part of the work. Testing, keeping a useful notebook, communicating decisions, and helping teammates make progress are engineering problems too.
