Model Aviation

Building and field-testing radio-controlled planes.

MY ROLE

MASC president and RC plane builder

FOCUS

Foam aircraft, 3D-printed designs, and flight testing

My role

I’m president of the Model Aviation Student Club at ASMSA. I work on club builds and built a radio-controlled model airplane.

Club builds

The club built and flew the foam Goliath, its largest aircraft at the time. In May 2026, the club flew its first custom-designed 3D-printed model, a pusher-prop aircraft.

What happened in testing

During one outing, an undersized electronic speed controller failed on a flight. The team replaced it and got the same plane flying again. On a later flight of the custom 3D-printed model, the plane took off but had a rough landing.
