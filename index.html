02 / A BIT ABOUT ME

Curiosity doesn’t
stay in one lane.

I like the space between an idea and something you can actually try.

I started building at nine. Since then, my interests have taken me from a Roblox financial-literacy game to hackathon prototypes, competition robots, and rocket simulation.

I skipped fourth and ninth grades and began junior year at the Arkansas School for Mathematics, Sciences, and the Arts at 14. Moving ahead has given me more room to chase the questions that interest me.

Robotics has also taught me that building things means building with people. I helped launch and captain Pinnacle View’s program, and I’m now helping rebuild the team at ASMSA.

BEYOND THE SCREEN
